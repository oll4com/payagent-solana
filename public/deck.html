The problem

  • An agent that works per task is paid by subscription or a manual checkout.
  • Nobody checks the task before the model spends money on it.
  • A customer who holds USDC on another chain has to bridge first.
  • Afterwards there is no record you can trust of who decided what.

One flow, checked at every step

  1. Taskdescription + price
  2. Policy gateAUTO · APPROVE · REFUSE, with the rule id
  3. Agent worksthe customer sees a preview
  4. Pay on Solanax402, or from Arbitrum / Base
  5. Result unlocksonly on a settled payment

Every step is appended to a hash-chained audit log: an edit or a deletion shows.

x402 on Solana (devnet)

GET /api/tasks/:id/result
→ 402 Payment Required
  scheme exact · solana-devnet · devnet USDC
  amount = the task's price · payTo = the owner
  • The wallet signs a USDC transfer; the facilitator verifies, settles and pays the fee.
  • A cent-sized price works: the fee is a fraction of a cent.
  • Failed payments: wrong amount, asset, receiver or a broken header → a new 402 with the reason, PAYMENT_REFUSED in the log, the task stays payable.

Any chain in, Solana USDC out

  1. Quote: USDC on Arbitrum, Base (or Ethereum, Optimism, Polygon, Avalanche) → USDC on Solana for the owner. Deposit address, minimum in, minimum out, refund address, deadline.
  2. Deposit from any wallet or exchange.
  3. Status polled: PENDING → PROCESSING → SUCCESS.
  4. Settlement checks before unlocking: deposit ≥ minimum in, delivery ≥ minimum out, and Solana itself shows the credit.

No testnet exists for Aurora: the flow runs on mainnet with small deposits.

A failed swap is refunded, and recorded

  • Pay less than the quote's minimum: INCOMPLETE_DEPOSIT, then REFUNDED to the customer's address when the quote closes.
  • The task stays payable; the deposit, the refunded amount, the reason and the refund transactions are kept in the task's history and the audit log.
  • A quote with a deposit in flight is never replaced.
  • Aurora reports success but the amounts do not match the quote: the task stays locked and AURORA_SETTLEMENT_REJECTED is logged.
