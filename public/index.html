PayAgent on Solana

An AI agent you pay per task. A policy gate decides before any work runs; the result unlocks only when the payment settles on Solana: with x402 (devnet) or from Arbitrum or Base through Aurora Intents. Every step goes to a tamper-evident audit log.

Source (MIT) · Slides · Encode Solana Hackathon 2026: Aurora Intents, Best Use of x402 on Solana

1. Give the agent a task

Tasks up to 1 USDC and 1,000 characters run at once; larger ones wait for a reviewer. Paying with x402 uses worthless devnet USDC. Paying from another chain moves real USDC: use a small price.

Pay with x402 on Solana devnet

The full result is an x402 resource. Without a payment it answers 402 Payment Required with an exact requirement: devnet USDC to the owner. A wallet signs a transfer, a facilitator verifies and settles it, and only a settled transaction unlocks the result.


        
Any x402 client pays the same route: node --env-file=.env scripts/devnet-pay.mjs <result URL>.

      


      

        
Pay from another chain with Aurora Intents

        
Paying from another chain is not set up on this server.

        

          
This path moves real USDC on mainnet. You send USDC on Arbitrum One or Base to a deposit address; Aurora swaps it into USDC on Solana for the owner. Send only USDC on the chosen chain.

          
          
          
          
          
          
          
If the swap fails or you send less than the minimum, Aurora refunds to this address when the quote closes, minus a small refund fee.
